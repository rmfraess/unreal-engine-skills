# Measure rendered garment clearance in PIE

1. Hold the actual player at a fixed animation time, let the cloth settle, and lock the camera and LOD. Capture front and side silhouettes with and without the garment. Use the original unmasked body material on the transient player; the outfit's skin mask removes the very surface being measured.
2. Use one `SceneCaptureComponent2D` with `PRM_USE_SHOW_ONLY_LIST`, `SCS_SCENE_DEPTH` and an `RTF_RGBA32F` target. For each surface (body, then cloth), call `clear_show_only_components()`, `show_only_component(component)`, `capture_scene()`, allow the capture to render, then read red with `RenderingLibrary.read_render_target_raw(world, target, False)`. Keep projection and transform identical within a pair.
3. Compute `body_depth - cloth_depth` at corresponding pixels: positive means the fabric is nearer the camera. Use named interior patches; exclude background, openings and occluded areas. Compare the median and P90 on pixels valid in both variants, and inspect the silhouette and local clipping separately. Report camera-axis depth in cm, not shortest surface distance or a whole-garment maximum.
4. If the posed mesh fits but the rendered shirt balloons, test the same cut with simulation off/on before altering the pattern again. Recheck the accepted chest fit after local sleeve or tuck edits, then watch a reach, release and stop; static clearance does not establish fabric weight.
